 05 transactions

 Eleven transaction types. The discriminant is the first byte of the payload and it is what decides the type.

 Minimum fees are per type. Percentage fees apply on top of the floor for the operations listed, so unit economics are the floor plus a percentage rather than the floor alone.

 canonical unsigned encoding

 Field | Bytes | Endianness | Offset |

 version | 1 | n/a | 0 |

 from | 32 | n/a | 1 |

 pubkey | 32 | n/a | 33 |

 nonce | 8 | little | 65 |

 fee | 8 | little | 73 |

 payload_len | 4 | little | 81 |

 payload | variable | n/a | 85 |

 The signature is 64 bytes appended after the payload. Fixed overhead is 149 bytes, which is TX_V1_OVERHEAD.

 signing

 Two details a non-Rust client has to get exactly right. The envelope is little-endian while payload internals are big-endian. And the signing preimage and the transaction id are different preimages: the signature covers a domain tag followed by the unsigned encoding, while the id is a hash of the unsigned encoding alone. Trailing bytes after the signature are a decode error, not padding.
